Step 1:Compile the Verilog design + testbench

	>>iverilog -o sim/simulation fsm_vending_machine.v tb_fsm_vending_machine.v

Step 2:Run the simulation

	>>vvp sim/simulation

This executes the compiled simulation.

Step 3:Generate a waveform

Your testbench should contain:

	//verilog
	$dumpfile("vending_machine.vcd");
	$dumpvars(0, tb_fsm_vending_machine);

Creates the waveform file: vending_machine.vcd

Step 4:Compile + simulate again

	>>iverilog -o sim/simulation fsm_vending_machine.v tb_fsm_vending_machine.v

	Then:

	>>vvp sim/simulation

Step 5:Open the waveform with GTKWave

	>>gtkwave vending_machine.vcd
