Step 1:Start yosys

	>>yosys

	yosys> read_verilog fsm_vending_machine.v

	yosys> hierarchy -top fsm_vending_machine

Step 2:synthesize

	yosys> synth

	yosys> stat

Step 3:Generate synthesized netlist

	yosys> write_verilog vending_netlist.v

This creates vending_netlist.v

Step 4:Exit

	yosys> exit

Step 5:Generate RTL schematic

	>>yosys
	yosys> read_verilog fsm_vending_machine.v
	yosys> hierarchy -top fsm_vending_machine
	yosys> proc
	yosys> opt
	yosys> fsm
	yosys> opt
	yosys> show -format dot -prefix vending_rtl
	yosys> exit

Now you have vending_rtl.dot

Step 6:save it as image

	>>dot -Tpng vending_rtl.dot -o vending_rtl.png

Now you have vending_rtl.png
